    Route assignment per classroom

    The exit most students in each room used. Changed routes are highlighted.

    Model: cellular-automaton crowd simulation on a 48 × 30 grid (1 cell ≈ 1 m, 1 step ≈ 1 s, walking ≈ 1 m/s, stairs ≈ 0.55 m/s). Standard plan follows the pre-drilled nearest-exit route and re-routes only after seeing fire or a blockage. NAJAT plan uses a risk-aware Dijkstra shortest path (edge cost rises near fire, cells touching fire are near-forbidden) and searches what-if exit assignments per classroom by simulating each one, keeping the plan with the lowest time and exposure. Routes are re-planned whenever the fire spreads. Based on Dijkstra (1959) and Helbing et al. (2000).
