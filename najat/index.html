    Method — Cellular-automaton crowd model on a 48 × 30 grid (1 cell ≈ 1 m, 1 step ≈ 1 s; stairs ≈ 0.55 m/s). The standard plan follows the drilled nearest-exit route and re-routes only after seeing fire or a blockage. NAJAT uses a risk-aware Dijkstra shortest path and simulates what-if exit assignments per classroom, keeping the safest, fastest plan; it keeps the drill plan when nothing beats it. References: Dijkstra (1959); Helbing et al. (2000).
